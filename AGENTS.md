# EdgeGrasp Replay

Independent offline replay product. The primary checkout is this repository;
the existing EdgeGrasp research checkouts and recordings are read-only sources.
Use normalized replay packages, not absolute paths or robot mesh downloads in public samples.
Never start robot controllers or physical experiments as a side effect of replay.
AI may propose shot plans; validate plans before deterministic Blender export.
Keep recorded, interpolated, synthetic and AI-authored information distinguishable.
Tests must cover malformed input, missing transforms, provenance and output round trips.
No secrets or source-machine paths in release artifacts. No claims of live Astra
execution or contest submission without actual evidence.
